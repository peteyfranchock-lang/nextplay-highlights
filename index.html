The mission

More than a highlight reel.

Recruiting is about more than one great play. NextPlay is designed to help athletes show coaches who they are, what they have accomplished, and what they can bring to a team.

This project focuses on simple navigation, a clear athlete story, and making it easy to move from highlights to contact information.

Built around three things coaches want to know:

✓ What can this athlete do?
✓ What has this athlete accomplished?
✓ How can I learn more?
