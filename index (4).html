01 / About me

SPORTS HAVE ALWAYS BEEN MY WORLD.

My name is Peter Franchock, and I am a freshman at Ithaca College, where I am a member of the basketball team. I have loved sports my entire life, and basketball has taught me the importance of working hard, staying committed, and being part of a team.

Being a student-athlete has also made me think about how players share their experiences with others. A highlight video can show a great play, but it does not always show the person behind it. That idea inspired NextPlay Highlights, a website concept where athletes can bring their videos and personal stories together. I am interested in learning more about sports, communication, and digital design, and this project gives me a chance to combine those interests while developing new skills.
