NextPlay Highlights

Created by Peter Franchock | Ithaca College

About the Project

NextPlay Highlights is a sports website I created to help athletes showcase their highlights, share their personal stories, and get noticed by college coaches. As a freshman basketball player at Ithaca College, sports have always been a huge part of my life. I wanted to create something that combines my passion for sports with technology.

Website Features

Athlete Profiles: Players can create profiles with their name, school, sport, and position.

Highlight Videos: Athletes can add highlight links or preview videos.

Tell Your Story: Players can share their background, accomplishments, and goals.

Athlete Showcase: A section to display athlete profiles.

Personal Portfolio: Information about me, my projects, skills, and interests.

About Me

My name is Peter Franchock, and I am a freshman at Ithaca College, where I play on the basketball team. I have loved sports my entire life and enjoy being part of a team. I created NextPlay Highlights because I understand how important it is for athletes to have opportunities to show their abilities and share their stories.

How I Built It

I created this website using AI-assisted coding as part of my Personal Portfolio Website project. I focused on making the website easy to navigate, visually appealing, and useful for athletes and coaches.

The website uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and is hosted using GitHub Pages.

Important Note

NextPlay Highlights is currently a demonstration website. Athlete profiles and video previews work in the browser, but submissions are not uploaded to a public database or shared with other visitors.
